# Guy Lines

Support cables used to guy antenna towers and masts at multiple stations.

**Materials:** Cotton and polypropylene blend

## Usage

Guy lines are used to stabilize and support antenna towers at:
- 15m Phone Station (with water bag anchors)
- 20m Phone Station (with water bag anchors)
- GOTA Station (with tree trunk anchors)

## Maintenance & Storage

- Inspect for fraying, wear, or damage
- Check for knots and integrity of attachment points
- Verify no rot or mildew in cotton sections
- Store in dry location, protected from UV exposure
- Keep organized and separated by station
