## Installation & Maintenance

- Verify mounting hardware is secure before each deployment
- Inspect control cables for damage or corrosion
- Test rotation function and indicator accuracy
- Lubricate bearings as per manufacturer guidance
- Store in protected location between events
- Keep controller dry and protected from weather
