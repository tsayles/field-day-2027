# General Supplies

Consumables and miscellaneous items needed at multiple stations.

## Common Items

### Cables & Cabling
- Coaxial cables (RG-8, RG-58, etc.)
- Mic cables
- Speaker cables
- Network cables

### Hardware
- Connectors (PL-259, BNC, RJ-45, etc.)
- Cable ties and velcro straps
- Zip ties
- Tape (electrical, gaffer, duct)

### Tools
- Flashlights and headlamps
- Multi-tools
- Screwdrivers and pliers
- Soldering equipment (as needed)

### Consumables
- Batteries (various sizes)
- Water bottles
- Snacks
- First aid supplies

### Documentation
- Station logs and operating sheets
- Checklists (setup, teardown, operating)
- Contact information and emergency procedures
