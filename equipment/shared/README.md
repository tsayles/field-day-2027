# Shared Equipment

Common items duplicated across most or all operating stations, plus tower and antenna support equipment.

Items in this folder are used at multiple stations (15m, 20m, 40m, Digital, GOTA, Satellite) at the Bluff site.

## Categories

### [Tents](tents.md)
CORE Instant Cabin Tent (14' x 9', 3-room) for shelter at operating stations.

### [Tables](tables.md)
Costco-style 6' folding tables for work surfaces and equipment placement.

### [Chairs](chairs.md)
Folding chairs for operator and support staff seating.

### [Power & Connectivity](power-connectivity.md)
Power distribution, cables, surge protection, and network equipment shared across stations.

**Includes:**
- [Station Distribution Cables](power-cables.md) — 10/3 AWG power cables from equipment trailer to each station

### Tower & Antenna Support

- [Rohn Tower Segments](rohn-tower.md) — Tapered top Rohn tower sections for 20m station antenna mounting
- [Tower Rotator](rotator.md) — Antenna rotator shared by 15m and 20m stations
- [Guy Lines](guy-lines.md) — Cotton and polypropylene support cables (15m, 20m, GOTA)
- [Water Bag Anchors](water-bag-anchors.md) — Ground anchors for guy lines (15m and 20m stations)

### [General Supplies](general-supplies.md)
Common consumables and miscellaneous items needed at multiple stations.
