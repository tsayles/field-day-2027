# Power & Connectivity

Power distribution, cables, surge protection, and network equipment shared across stations.

## Station Power Distribution

### [Station Distribution Cables](power-cables.md)
Each operating station receives power via a dedicated cable running from the main equipment trailer:
- **Cable:** 10/3 AWG
- **Source (Trailer):** NEMA 5-20P (20-amp 120V)
- **Station End:** Quad box with 4× NEMA 5-15R (15-amp 120V)
- **Lengths:** Vary by station location (to be documented)

### Per-Station Power
- Station-level surge protection
- Extension cords and power strips (as needed)
- Cable management and strain relief

## Network Equipment

### Ethernet/Internet
- Shared network backbone (if available at site)
