# Tables

Folding work tables used for equipment and operator work surfaces across stations.

## Costco Style 6' Folding Tables

**Type:** Commercial-grade folding table  
**Length:** 6 feet  
**Height:** 30 inches (standard work height)  
**Surface:** Durable tabletop suitable for equipment and supplies  

### Notes
- Lightweight and portable for easy setup/teardown
- Stackable for compact transport in trailers
- Used at most or all operating stations
- See [table leveling blocks](https://github.com/tsayles/field-day-2027/issues/18) for uneven terrain solutions

### Maintenance
- Check hinges and locking mechanisms before each event
- Inspect for cracks or damage to tabletop
- Clean and dry after use to prevent rust on metal components
