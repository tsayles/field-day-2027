### How It Works

Water bags serve as temporary ground anchors for guy lines, allowing the towers to be set up without permanent ground installation:
- Bags filled with water at site
- Guy lines attached to bag handles or anchor points
- Provides weight and stability for tower support
- Easy to deploy and remove

## Usage

- 15m Phone Station
- 20m Phone Station

## Setup & Teardown

### Setup
- Fill bags with fresh water to target weight
- Position bags at appropriate guy line angles
- Secure guy line attachment points
- Verify tension and stability before raising tower

### Teardown
- Drain bags completely before storage
- Dry bags thoroughly to prevent mold/mildew
- Inspect for cracks, leaks, or deterioration
- Store empty in dry location

## Maintenance & Storage

- Inspect bags regularly for UV damage, cracks, or leaks
- Verify attachment points are secure and not degraded
- Replace if water-tight integrity is compromised
- Store in protected, shaded location
- Keep separate from other equipment
