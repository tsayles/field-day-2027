# Power Distribution Cables (Station to Trailer)

Each operating station is supplied power via an individual distribution cable running from the main equipment trailer.

## Cable Specifications

**Wire Gauge:** 10/3 AWG  
**Connectors:**
- **Source End (Trailer):** NEMA 5-20P (male, 20-amp 120V)
- **Station End:** Quad box with 4× NEMA 5-15R (female, 15-amp 120V)

## Maintenance & Storage

- Inspect connectors for corrosion or damage before each event
- Check for cuts, abrasions, or deterioration in cable insulation
- Test connection integrity and ensure proper grounding
- Store coiled in dry location, away from direct sunlight
- Keep separate from other cables to prevent tangling
