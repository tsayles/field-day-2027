# Crank-Up Tower

The primary antenna support structure for the 15m Phone Station, permanently mounted on the tower trailer.

## Tower Specifications

**Type:** Crank-up tower (manually cranked vertical extension)  
**Mounting:** Permanently mounted to tower trailer  
**Antenna:** 15m Phone antenna  
**Height Extended:** TBD - to be confirmed  
**Height Collapsed:** TBD - to be confirmed  

### Operation

- **Extension/Retraction:** Manual crank system
- **Rotation:** Uses shared [Tower Rotator](../../../shared/rotator.md) for directional antenna control
- **Guy Lines:** Cotton and polypropylene blend (shared)
- **Anchors:** [Water Bag Anchors](../../../shared/water-bag-anchors.md) (shared with 20m station)

## Setup & Deployment

### Space Requirements
- **15'+ of flat space behind tower trailer** is required for safe antenna mounting/unmounting
- Adequate clearance for rotating antenna during deployment
- Safe work area for team members during assembly

### Landing Gear Stabilization
The tower trailer uses jack-style landing gear with **cribbing requirements**:
- Use solid cribbing blocks under all landing gear points
- Verify level, stable support before cranking tower up
- Distribute weight evenly across all supports

## Maintenance & Inspection

- Check crank mechanism for smooth operation
- Inspect cable or drive system for damage or wear
- Verify mounting bolts and brackets are tight
- Test rotation range and limits before each deployment
- Lubricate mechanical components as per manufacturer guidance
