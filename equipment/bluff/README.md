# Bluff (Battery Grattan)

Base of operations for Field Day 2027. This is the central hub where the Phone, Digital, GOTA, and Satellite stations operate, plus the Visitor Tent.

## Equipment Subfolders

### equipment-trailer/
Main equipment trailer for the operation.

**Related Repository:**  
🔗 [mk-equip-trailer](https://github.com/tsayles/mk-equip-trailer) — Detailed inventory, maintenance logs, and documentation for the equipment trailer.

### tower-trailer/
Tower trailer and associated equipment.

### other/
Miscellaneous equipment and resources at the Bluff site.

## Bluff-Specific Facilities

### [canopy-refreshment-station.md](canopy-refreshment-station.md)
Pop-up canopy shelter and refreshment area, typically set up over park-owned picnic tables near the equipment trailer. Includes large igloo-style water jug for volunteer hydration.

### [portable-facilities.md](portable-facilities.md)
Rented portable toilets and portable sink for operator and volunteer sanitation facilities.
