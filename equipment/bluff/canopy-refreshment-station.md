# Pop-Up Canopy & Refreshment Station

Station shelter and refreshment area located at the Bluff, typically set up over park-owned picnic tables near the equipment trailer.

## Equipment

### Pop-Up Canopy
- **Type:** Portable pop-up canopy shelter
- **Placement:** Over park-owned picnic tables near equipment trailer
- **Purpose:** Shade and weather protection for refreshment/break area

### Igloo-Style Water Jug
- **Type:** Large insulated water cooler (igloo-style)
- **Location:** At canopy station
- **Purpose:** Provides drinking water for volunteers and operators
- **Maintenance:** Keep filled and clean throughout Field Day

## Setup Notes

- Position canopy to provide shade over picnic table work surface
- Secure canopy against wind with guy lines or stakes
- Place water jug on or near table for easy access
- Keep area clean and organized for volunteer breaks

## Maintenance & Storage

- Inspect canopy for damage to fabric and frame before each deployment
- Verify all frame connections are secure and functional
- Check water jug for leaks or cracks; drain and dry completely after use
- Store in protected location between events
- Replace water jug if structural integrity is compromised
