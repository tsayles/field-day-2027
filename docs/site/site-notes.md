# Fort Flagler — Site Notes

## Location
Fort Flagler State Park
172 Flagler Campground Rd, Nordland, WA 98358
Marrowstone Island, Jefferson County

Operations area: **Battery Gratten**, northeast corner of the park.
Public info booth located at Battery Gratten entrance.

[Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UbZ4qKZAgaVeCazFA)

- Automated pay station at park entrance (1-day or annual pass).
