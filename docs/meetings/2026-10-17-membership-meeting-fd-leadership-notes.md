## Context & Motivation

Last year's Field Day (2026) experienced **personnel challenges at the last minute**, including loss of the director and other key roles. The board wants to ensure this doesn't happen again by:
- Building **organizational depth** and backup roles
- Getting volunteer commitments **early** (not June 1st)
- Using a structured volunteer coordination system

## Key Discussion Points & Decisions

### 1. **Double Safety Officers** (David's suggestion)
- Fort Flagler has beach and plateau areas that are geographically separated
- Recommend **two safety officers** to cover both zones simultaneously
- Ensures someone can take breaks while maintaining coverage
- **Status:** Proposal accepted

### 2. **Contingency Planning for Setup** (David's suggestion)
- Physical capabilities of volunteer crew will vary year-to-year
- Need **backup plans** based on who shows up (e.g., 30-ft tower → 20-meter fiberglass mast)
- Emphasizes importance of getting younger volunteers involved
- **Status:** Principle accepted; implement in planning

### 3. **Band Chair Role Clarification** (Robert - 20M Chair)
- **Current model:** Band chairs handle only operating positions  
- **Proposed model:** Band chairs act as **coordinators** (not workers), delegating antenna/equipment tasks to pre-recruited volunteers
- **Debate:** Robert expressed concern about adding antenna/equipment duties on top of operator recruitment burden
- **Resolution:** Band chairs will:
  - Focus on operator recruitment and team-building (pre-planned)
  - Identify specific volunteer needs and surface blockers to director/leadership
  - Feed needs into centralized **volunteer system** for better tracking and recruitment
  - Coordinate equipment work parties (antenna maintenance, cleaning, etc.) at Radio Camp or late May/early June
  - **Status:** Approach clarified; band chairs as **coordinators** (not additional workers)

### 4. **Youth & Community Engagement** (David's suggestion)
- Bring in younger workers (Scouts, STEM clubs, high school groups)
- They get experience, badge credits, and energy for setup work
- Requires someone to build these relationships now
- **Recommendation:** Designate "Non-Member Volunteer Coordinator" role to handle outreach
- **Status:** New role proposed

### 5. **Antenna Maintenance Ahead of Time** (David's suggestion)
- Bring equipment trailer to **Radio Camp** (June 21–24, 2027) for early maintenance
- Service, clean, and lubricate antennas before Field Day
- Document antenna setup procedures (David has photos from 2026; 20M was set up backwards twice)
- **Status:** Practice approved; coordinate with equipment lead

### 6. **Volunteer Recognition** (Presenter)
- Propose **hats or t-shirts** for volunteers (similar to past MKARC events)
- Improves morale and creates continuity
- **Status:** Recommendation to board pending

## Recruitment & Engagement Strategy

### Key Challenge Identified
- Club has ~300 members but low field day participation (same ~20-25% "core group" each year)
- Email/newsletter announcements have **low impact**
- Need **direct personal contact** to increase volunteer engagement

### Recommended Approaches

1. **Board & Officer Phone Tree** (starting February 2027)
   - Personal phone calls (not emails) to members who've expressed FD interest
   - "How can we get you involved?" conversations
   - Much higher success rate than impersonal outreach
   - Escalate in frequency as June approaches

2. **Volunteer System** (Hal's swap-meet tool)
   - Centralized sign-up system for all roles
   - Better visibility into role needs and commitments
   - Makes it easier to match volunteers with specific tasks
   - Should be launched **now**, not June 1st

3. **Membership Application Follow-up** (Gregor - online)
   - Many applications have "field day" or other interests checked
   - Need systematic process to follow up with new members
   - Create engagement pipeline for interested members
