# Field Day 2027 Volunteer Sign-Up Roles

**Source:** [MKARC Field Day 2027 Volunteer Sign-Up](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdNkSygRRDbv0HmI0SfuBtOZ7w1RTAnQGbve2HpOGIMs1-4OA/viewform)

## Critical Leadership Roles

- [ ] **Field Day 2027 Director** — Overall FD operation lead; final decision authority
- [ ] **Assistant Director / Field Day Assistant** — Supports the director, keeps staffing and task visibility, and serves as backup leadership as needed
- [ ] **Safety Officer #1** — On-site safety authority; performs safety checks, enforces site safety rules, and can halt unsafe activities
- [ ] **Safety Officer #2** — On-site safety authority; performs safety checks, enforces site safety rules, and can halt unsafe activities

---

## Coordination Roles (Leadership)

- [ ] **Friday Potluck Coordinator** — Organize Friday evening potluck at Wagon Wheel Campground
- [ ] **Welcome Station Coordinator** — Lead welcome/check-in station activities
- [ ] **Points Chair** — Track and manage Field Day scoring/points
- [ ] **Equipment Chair** — Oversee equipment logistics and inventory
- [ ] **CW Beach Coordinator** — Lead CW (Morse code) station setup and activities
- [ ] **15 Meter Band Coordinator** — Lead 15m operating station
- [ ] **20 Meter Band Coordinator** — Lead 20m operating station
- [ ] **40 Meter Band Coordinator** — Lead 40m operating station
- [ ] **Digital Station Coordinator** — Lead digital modes station

## Setup & Logistics

### Radio Camp Prep (Starting Monday of Radio Camp)
- [ ] Assist with Wagon Wheel Campground coordination and site prep

### Friday Setup (Pre-Field Day)
- [ ] General setup crew — assist with staging equipment, running cables, setting up tents, and other site prep tasks
- [ ] Tower setup ground crew — more physical work, including tower and antenna setup/grounding tasks

- [ ] Assist with Friday evening potluck at Wagon Wheel Campground
- [ ] Coordinate potluck (leadership role — see Coordination Roles above)

- [ ] **Operator** — Operate CW station during Field Day (11 AM Saturday – 11 AM Sunday)
- [ ] **Assistant** — Assist with CW Beach activities

### Phone Station
- [ ] **Operator** — Operate phone station during Field Day (11 AM Saturday – 11 AM Sunday)

### Get On The Air (GOTA) Station
- [ ] **Coordinator** — Lead GOTA station (leadership role — see Coordination Roles above)
- [ ] **Assistant** — Assist with GOTA station activities
