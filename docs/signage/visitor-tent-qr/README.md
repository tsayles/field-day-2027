# Visitor Tent QR Signage (Issue #1)

This folder contains QR-code signage assets for the Field Day visitor tent.

## Deliverables

- `field-day-visitor-qr-logo.svg` — QR design asset with centered M&K logo.
- `field-day-visitor-qr-sign-letter.svg` — print-ready 8.5x11 sign for long-term use at the visitor tent.

## Intended destination

The QR code points visitors to the club Field Day page:

- https://mikeandkey.org/fday1.php

## Print guidance (long-term use)

- Print on letter size (8.5x11 inch) at high quality.
- Use UV/weather-resistant sleeve or laminate for repeated outdoor use.
- Keep the sign flat and unwarped to preserve QR scannability.
- Place at visitor-table eye level with good lighting and minimal glare.
