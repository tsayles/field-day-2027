## Equipment & Antennas

**E1** ✅ — What antennas were used on each band in 2026, and how did they
perform?

*Phone stations (on the bluff):*
- 15m & 20m: 4-element Yagi on towers
- 40m: Two 4-element inverted-V Yagi antennas suspended between
  treetops — one points east, one points south

*CW stations (on the beach):*
- 15m, 20m, 40m: Various verticals; saltwater provides an excellent
  ground plane

*Digital (on the bluff):* Crank-IR multi-band vertical

*GOTA (on the bluff):* StepIR 3-element Yagi on a tower

*Satellite (on the bluff):* 2m/70cm Yagi on a tripod gimbal mount

**E2** ✅ — Which radios are club-owned vs. member-provided? Is there a plan
to standardize?

Most radios are member-provided. Band captains choose the radios for
their station. No standardization plan currently in place.

**E3** ✅ — Were there any RF interference (RFI) issues between stations in
2026? If so, which band pairs conflicted?

The digital station is located near and below the 20m tower (constrained
by terrain and logistics). When the digital station transmitted, 20m was
completely blocked. Workaround: digital station avoids 20m when other
bands are active. No other inter-station RFI issues reported.

*Action item: investigate a longer feedline run or band-pass filtering
to give digital station more separation from the 20m tower.*

**E4** ✅ — What is the condition of club-owned coax, connectors, and feed
lines? Is an inventory/inspection needed?

Most coax feed lines and power cables are in good condition. Known
problem areas:
- Rotator cables and connectors (ongoing issues)
- StepIR control cable
- Rotators themselves may need bench testing before 2027

**E5** ✅ — Do we have a backup radio for any station in case of equipment
failure mid-event?

Backup radio availability is limited and depends on what members bring
to "Radio Camp." No formal backup plan exists.

*Radio Camp:* The club reserves the Wagon Wheel group camp site for the
full week (Monday to Monday). Members who arrive early use the week to
set up, test, tune, and operate — as well as other radio activities.

**E6** ✅ — What amplifiers (if any) are used? Are they within the power
limits for Class 6A?

All stations operate at 100W or less. Amplifiers are deployed as needed
within legal limits.
