## Agenda (60 minutes)

### 1️⃣ Sprint Review (10 min)
Review completed tasks from previous sprint.

**Focus questions:**
- What did we finish last sprint?
- What blockers did we hit?
- Any wins to celebrate?

### 2️⃣ Backlog Grooming (20 min)
Discuss upcoming sprint work; refine GitHub issues.

**Items to review:**
- [Link to GitHub issues/project board for this sprint]
- New feedback or requests to triage

### 3️⃣ Sprint Planning (15 min)
Assign work and set sprint goals.

**Decisions needed:**
- What are we committing to this sprint?
- Who owns which tasks?
- Any blockers or dependencies?

### 4️⃣ Open Discussion (10 min)
Questions, blockers, dependencies.

**Discuss:**
- Anything else on your mind?
- Concerns or help needed?

### 5️⃣ Decisions & Commitments (5 min)
Confirm who's doing what and next steps.

**Confirm:**
- Assigned tasks and owners
- Next meeting date and focus
- Anything we forgot to cover
